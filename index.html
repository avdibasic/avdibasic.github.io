        Pentesting Approach

        How an engagement runs end to end. Each phase feeds the next, from agreeing scope to handing over findings a team can act on.

        1. Phase 01 Scoping & Rules of Engagement

          Agree the targets, timing, constraints and legal authorisation up front, and define what a successful engagement looks like.

        2. Phase 02 Reconnaissance & OSINT

          Map the public attack surface and gather intelligence from open sources before touching anything internal.

        3. Phase 03 Enumeration & Scanning

          Identify live hosts, services, versions and misconfigurations across everything in scope.

        4. Phase 04 Exploitation & Initial Access

          Turn a validated weakness into a foothold, carefully and strictly within the agreed scope.

        5. Phase 05 Post-Exploitation

          Escalate privileges, move laterally and work toward the objective, such as Domain Admin or Tier 0, documenting every step.

        6. Phase 06 Reporting & Remediation

          Write findings with impact, evidence and reproducible steps, then retest once fixes are in place.
